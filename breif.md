# REAPER HQ — Website Design & Content Brief

**For the website designer**

## 1. Brand Identity

**Business name:** Reaper HQ  
**Business type:** Martial Arts Academy  
**Primary disciplines:** Muay Thai • Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu  
**Additional training:** MMA, wrestling, fitness, and self-defense  
**Location:** West Palm Beach, Florida

**Brand positioning:** Reaper HQ is a community-focused martial arts academy offering high-quality training for children, teenagers, adults, beginners, hobbyists, and competitive athletes.

We want to combine legitimate martial arts instruction with a welcoming environment where people can develop confidence, discipline, fitness, and practical skills.

**Our main message:**
You do not need to be a fighter to train like one. Whether someone is a parent looking for a positive activity for their child, a beginner looking to get in shape, or an experienced martial artist, Reaper HQ should feel like a place for them.

**Brand name recommendation:** Use REAPER HQ as the primary brand across the website. Use “Martial Arts Academy” as the descriptive subtitle and “Muay Thai • Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu” to communicate our core disciplines.

## 2. Website Goals

The website should help us:

1. Get new students to sign up for their first class.
2. Help parents understand our children's and teen programs.
3. Explain our adult martial arts programs and beginner-friendly approach.
4. Generate leads and inquiries from homeschool families and schools.
5. Make our class schedule, location, and membership options easy to find.
6. Build credibility with professional branding, instructor bios, and clear safety standards.
7. Establish Reaper HQ as a trusted local martial arts community in West Palm Beach.

The most important action we want visitors to take is **Book a Trial Class** or **Contact Us to Get Started**.

## 3. Recommended Website Pages

### Home

The homepage should immediately communicate who we are, what we teach, who we serve, and how to get started.

Suggested headline:

**BUILD CONFIDENCE. DEVELOP DISCIPLINE. FIND YOUR STRENGTH.**

Suggested supporting text:

“Welcome to Reaper HQ, West Palm Beach's community-focused martial arts academy. We offer Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and MMA training for kids, teens, and adults — from complete beginners to experienced athletes. Build skills, improve fitness, and become part of a team that helps you grow.”

Primary buttons:

- Book a Trial Class
- Explore Our Programs

Include a preview of our programs, instructor introductions, schedule, location, and a clear contact section.

### Programs

Create a dedicated page for each major program or an organized page with separate sections.

**Reaper Cubs — Ages 4–6**

- Fun, age-appropriate martial arts activities.
- Coordination, balance, and movement.
- Introductory striking and grappling fundamentals.
- Basic self-defense and personal safety.
- Listening skills, confidence, discipline, and teamwork.
- Games that make learning enjoyable.

**Little Reapers — Ages 7–11**

- Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai fundamentals.
- Age-appropriate self-defense.
- Anti-bullying awareness and personal safety.
- Coordination, fitness, and athletic development.
- Respect, confidence, discipline, and teamwork.
- A structured pathway toward more advanced training.

**Teen Program — Ages 12–15**

- Muay Thai and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu fundamentals.
- Practical self-defense and controlled technical training.
- Strength, conditioning, coordination, and fitness.
- Confidence, accountability, and discipline.
- Opportunities to progress toward advanced training where appropriate.

**Adult Programs**

- Beginner Muay Thai.
- Beginner Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.
- Intermediate training.
- Advanced and competition-oriented training, when available.
- MMA training and cross-training opportunities.
- Fitness, stress relief, self-defense, and skill development.

Make it clear that beginners are welcome and that students do not need prior martial arts experience to start.

### Muay Thai

Explain the benefits of learning striking, including technique, footwork, conditioning, coordination, confidence, and self-defense.

Emphasize proper instruction, structured progression, and training appropriate to each student's experience level.

### Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Explain grappling, positional control, escapes, submissions, problem-solving, and practical self-defense.

Highlight that BJJ can benefit both people who want to compete and people who simply want to learn, exercise, and build confidence.

### MMA & Advanced Training

Present MMA as a way to combine striking, grappling, wrestling, and transitions between disciplines.

Keep competition training distinct from beginner and recreational programs so new visitors do not feel intimidated.

### Homeschool & Community Programs

We want to develop opportunities for homeschool families, schools, and community organizations.

Potential offerings include:

- Homeschool martial arts and physical education.
- Movement, fitness, and coordination.
- Muay Thai and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu instruction.
- Self-defense and personal safety.
- Teamwork, discipline, and confidence.
- School enrichment and after-school partnerships, as available.

We are also interested in exploring Florida Step Up scholarship participation. Do not advertise scholarship acceptance or guaranteed eligibility until our provider status and applicable program requirements have been confirmed.

### About Us

Tell the story behind Reaper HQ.

We want to build a place where martial arts are accessible to the community, not exclusively to competitive fighters. Our mission is to help people of different ages and experience levels become stronger, more confident, and more capable through martial arts.

Emphasize:

- Authentic martial arts experience.
- High-quality instruction.
- A welcoming atmosphere.
- Youth development and positive mentorship.
- Community involvement.
- Training for health, confidence, personal growth, and competition.

### Schedule & Memberships

Include an easy-to-read weekly schedule organized by age group, discipline, and experience level.

Display membership prices, class availability, family options, and any applicable discounts once finalized.

### Contact & Location

Include:

- Full gym address once the lease/location details are finalized.
- Interactive map and directions.
- Phone number and business email.
- Contact form.
- Social media links.
- Trial-class booking or inquiry form.
- Parking and arrival instructions, if useful.

## 4. Brand Personality & Visual Design

The website should feel **strong, professional, modern, and welcoming**.

We want the Reaper identity to stand out without making parents or beginners think the gym is hostile or exclusively for fighters.

**Visual direction:**

- Dark, clean backgrounds with strong contrast.
- Black, white, and restrained accent colors that complement the Reaper HQ logo.
- Bold, readable typography.
- Professional photos of real training, instructors, kids' classes, and adults.
- Clear separation between youth programs and advanced fight training.
- Subtle Reaper-inspired graphics and branding rather than excessive skulls or aggressive imagery.

The overall impression should be a serious martial arts academy with a strong identity and a positive community.

## 6. Memberships & Pricing

Our working pricing concepts have included:

- Single-discipline adult membership: approximately $150/month.
- Full-access adult membership: approximately $180–$200/month.
- Children's membership: approximately $150/month.
- Homeschool program: approximately $100/month for one or two weekly classes.
- Family memberships.
- Discounts for veterans, active military, paramedics, and law enforcement.

These are planning figures, not necessarily final published prices. Confirm the current prices, inclusions, eligibility, and terms before posting them on the website.

## 8. Search Engine Optimization

The website should help people in our area find us when they search for services such as:

- Martial arts in West Palm Beach, FL.
- Kids' martial arts in West Palm Beach.
- Children's Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.
- Kids' Muay Thai.
- Adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.
- Beginner Muay Thai classes.
- MMA training in West Palm Beach.
- Homeschool martial arts and physical education.

Use these phrases naturally in page titles, headings, descriptions, and relevant program pages. Avoid keyword stuffing or claims that cannot be supported.

## 9. Marketing & Growth

Our marketing strategy can include social media, referrals, former students, homeschool communities, school partnerships, and local community connections.

The website should support our initial student enrollment goals and our longer-term plan to grow into a well-established academy.

We want the site to make it easy for parents, adults, and community partners to contact us and understand what makes our academy different.

## 11. The Overall Vision

Reaper HQ should become more than a place to learn how to fight. We want it to be a place where children develop confidence and discipline, teenagers build positive habits, adults improve their health and skills, and experienced martial artists continue to grow.

The website should communicate three things immediately:

**LEGITIMATE MARTIAL ARTS. WELCOMING TO EVERYONE. BUILT AROUND COMMUNITY.**

We want a polished, easy-to-use website that makes people feel comfortable taking the first step and proud to become part of the Reaper HQ team.

There are five things I would prioritize:

1. A strong homepage with a prominent Book a Trial Class button.
2. A dedicated kids' section that makes it easy for parents to understand the age groups and benefits.
3. A clear class schedule that is easy to view on a phone.
4. Simple membership and registration options so interested people can become students without a complicated process.
5. A professional brand presentation that balances the Reaper identity with your welcoming, community-first mission.
